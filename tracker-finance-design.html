Reasoning, written down (R-31)

  • Green means money in, nothing else. Income amounts and the total balance carry the one green accent (Monarch's "green for money" cue). Expenses stay in ink with a leading minus, transfers stay neutral: colour marks direction, never decoration.
  • Red is danger, not "expense". An everyday expense is neutral; red appears only on Void and Over budget, and always with the words next to it plus a hatch, so status never rides on colour alone (R-25, UX-FLOWS).
  • One big Fraunces number. The total balance is the single editorial figure on the screen, the same identity motif used for the Dashboard's headline number and the Tasks section markers.
  • Drafts and transfers are excluded, in words. The line under the total says so, and a draft row is labelled "Draft, not in balance yet", matching the rule that drafts don't move the balance (SCHEMA).
  • Opening-balance correction is append-only. The modal shows the immutable change log and states it isn't income, mirroring FinanceAccountBalanceChange rather than overwriting a field.
  • Void keeps the record. The confirm step explains the balance recomputes and the row can't be edited or reposted; the voided row stays visible, struck through, not deleted.
  • Tabular numbers everywhere money appears. Inter tabular-nums aligns the IDR column (Monarch's tidy-number reference); Fraunces is kept for headings and the one total.
  • Sub-nav mirrors real routes. Accounts, Transactions, and Budgets are the actual /finance/* pages, so no nav item points at a screen that doesn't exist (R-24).
